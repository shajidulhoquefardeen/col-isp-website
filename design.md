# Design System Specification: Corporate Connectivity (COL)

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
The Creative North Star for this design system is **"The Trusted Hub."** 

This system focuses on conveying reliability, technological sophistication, and professional connectivity. It uses a structured, grid-based layout with a clean, high-contrast color palette to establish authority and trust in the telecommunications and ISP sector.

## 2. Visual Language

### Color Palette
- **Primary Brand Color:** Deep Navy (#002147) - used for headers, primary buttons, and significant UI sections to convey stability and professionalism.
- **Secondary Accent:** Vibrant Red (#D71920) - used sparingly for call-to-action highlights, logo accents (like the checkmark), and critical alerts.
- **Backgrounds:** 
  - Off-white/Cream (#F8F9FA) for main content areas to improve readability.
  - White (#FFFFFF) for cards and nested containers.
- **Text:** 
  - Primary: Deep Charcoal (#1C1D20) for maximum legibility.
  - Secondary/Meta: Medium Grey (#6C757D).

### Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** Clean Sans-Serif (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or Helvetica).
- **Hierarchy:** 
  - **Headers:** Bold weights with tight tracking. Used for major sections and service titles.
  - **Body:** Regular weight with generous line height (1.5 - 1.6) for readability.
  - **Functional UI:** Semi-bold for buttons and navigation items.

### Iconography & Imagery
- **Icons:** Thin-line or semi-bold glyphs contained within circular borders.
- **Imagery:** High-technology motifs, urban skylines with network overlays, and professional architectural photography.

## 3. Structural & UX Patterns
- **Grid System:** Strict 12-column grid for alignment.
- **Components:** 
  - **Service Cards:** Clean white cards with subtle drop shadows, featuring a central icon and concise description.
  - **Hero Section:** Large, impactful imagery with a central value proposition and clear CTAs.
  - **Navigation:** Horizontal top bar with dropdown menus for complex service hierarchies.

## 4. Tone of Voice
- **Professional**
- **Reliable**
- **Innovative**
- **Customer-Centric**
